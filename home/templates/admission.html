Admissions

Curriculum

We use National Curriculum for Nigerian Education

Gaga school follows an organized admission process with the following steps:

STEP 1:

Application: Forms are obtainable at the school or online on the school's website.

STEP 2:

Assessment: Applicant's take the assessment test at their with prior notification and booking with the admissions office.

STEP 3:

Interview: Applicants and their parents are interviewed by the Head of schools or Director to better understand the student's educational goals.

STEP 4:

Provisional Admission: Successful applicants are offered provisional admission until payment of the required fees.

STEP 5:

Admission: In order to guarantee a place at Gaga School, all fees must be paid by the date indicated on the provisional admission letter.

Note: Parents are advised on timely payment.
